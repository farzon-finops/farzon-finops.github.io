Case files

Four cases, how I handled them

Process redesign

Advance POs for major suppliers

Situation
Payments took about 7 days under the SOP. That hurt supplier relationships, and hotels in particular want payment right after a booking is confirmed.
What I did
  • Collected estimated spend from the business for each major supplier.
  • Got one-time approval from all approvers and raised Advance POs, replacing the 10–15 PRs raised daily.
  • Split suppliers into big (with agreement) and small (without agreement), and moved frequent urgent payments to Advance POs.
Outcome
Urgent payments go out the same day, and payment-delay escalations dropped.

Reconciliation

Corporate cards in SAP Concur

Situation
About 25 cards across 6 teams, 500 to 1,500 transactions and Rs. 10–50L a month. Invoice mismatches ran above 40%.
What I did
  • Collected and checked invoices, then uploaded them.
  • Put mismatches into one standard document with approval from each team's point of contact.
  • Cleared old expenses with no proof through one-time approval, and recharged cards on time.
Outcome
Mismatches fell from over 40% to about 2%.

Vendor management · SOP adherence

Vendor Management & Agreement Governance

SOP requires an agreement

Above Rs. 50 lakh

Small-vendor advances

Up to Rs. 10 lakh

Situation
Under the Finance and Legal SOP, agreements are mandatory for transactions exceeding Rs. 50 lakh. However, engagements with smaller vendors involving advances of up to Rs. 10 lakh can also expose the business to financial and contractual risks when written agreements are absent.
Process challenge
Once a vendor signs the agreement, the document must undergo review by five internal teams. Without structured follow-ups and timely clarification of outstanding issues, the approval process can stall, delaying agreement finalization and potentially affecting business operations.
My contribution
  • Agreement initiation: Initiated the applicable agreement process upon managerial instruction, in accordance with internal SOP requirements.
  • Vendor coordination: Communicated with vendors, shared company agreement drafts, and coordinated their review, consent and signatures.
  • Cross-functional collaboration: Coordinated agreement reviews with GST Compliance, Accounts Controller, Business, Business Finance and Legal teams.
  • Issue resolution: Tracked pending reviews, facilitated clarifications, coordinated issue resolution, and escalated delays where necessary.
  • Approval management: Facilitated the agreement through the prescribed internal approval workflow and coordinated submission for final sign-off.
Process improvement proposal
Proposed extending written agreement coverage to smaller-vendor engagements involving advance payments, recognizing that lower transaction values do not eliminate financial or contractual exposure. Formalizing agreed terms helps establish accountability, clarify obligations, mitigate potential disputes, and protect the company's business interests.
Outcome
Coordinated the agreement review and approval process for vendors with transaction values exceeding Rs. 50 lakh. Following implementation of the agreement process, purchase orders (POs) no longer required repeated approvals.

Vendor onboarding: domestic and foreign companies

Scope
New businesses brought on as vendors, both Indian and foreign companies. The vendor's country of origin decides which documents are needed.
What I did
